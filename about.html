What Is Untamed SMP?

Untamed SMP is a long-term survival world where players shape the story. Whether you're building a kingdom, creating a trading empire, exploring distant lands, or constructing massive projects, every player leaves a lasting mark.

The focus is freedom, creativity, community, and adventure. We want a world that feels alive and constantly evolving through player achievements and collaboration.

No matter your playstyle—builder, explorer, redstoner, merchant, or adventurer—there is a place for you in Untamed SMP.
